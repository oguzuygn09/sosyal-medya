Planning Cappadocia for a winter group? Here is what we always tell our partners.

→ Book two nights, not one. Balloon flights are confirmed each morning depending on the wind, so a second morning gives your clients a real second chance.
→ Pack for below zero. Sunrise in the valleys is cold, even when the afternoons are mild.
→ Treat snow as a bonus. The fairy chimneys look their best under fresh snow, and the cave hotels stay warm and quiet.

Winter is one of the most rewarding seasons in Cappadocia, as long as the itinerary leaves room for the weather.
